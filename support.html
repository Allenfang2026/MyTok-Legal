Send a description of the problem by email. There is no live-chat service or guaranteed response time advertised in this version.

For a purchase question, include your MyTok version, what happened, and the Support ID displayed in Settings → Membership if available. Your Support ID is not a password. Do not send your Apple password, private keys, payment-card information or personal media.

Restore a purchase

Open MyTok → Settings → Membership → Restore Purchases while using the Apple purchase account that bought the membership. Wait for verified access. A network failure is different from a confirmed absence of purchases; retry when connected. You do not need to register a MyTok account.

Manage a subscription

Use MyTok's Manage Subscription button or iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Uninstalling MyTok or buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Apple handles billing and refund requests through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Photo access and local notes

Change Photos access in iPhone Settings. MyTok only reads the media you authorize. Notes and tags are stored locally in this version; avoid reinstalling as a troubleshooting step when you need to preserve them. Provide an error description rather than private media.
