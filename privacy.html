MyTok Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Last revised: October 15, 2026
Operator: Ailun Fang (方艾伦), an individual developer
Contact: allenfangailun@outlook.com

MyTok helps you revisit photos and videos in your iPhone photo library. You do not need to create a MyTok account.

Photos, videos and local data

You choose the photo-library access granted to MyTok. The app reads the media you allow to provide browsing, favorites, sharing and the changes you request. Media stays in your photo library; MyTok does not upload it to our own servers or RevenueCat. Apple may download media from your own iCloud library according to your settings and the app's cellular-loading preference.

Notes, tags, exclusion preferences and trial-viewing records are stored on the device. This version does not provide MyTok cloud backup or cross-device synchronization of notes and tags. Local app data may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings. Reinstalling the app can remove local data. Changes to favorites and deletion of original media use Apple's Photos APIs and may synchronize through your Apple services. Photos manages Recently Deleted and recovery.

Saved shooting locations

To display a city, MyTok can send a photo or video's saved shooting coordinates to Apple's place-name service. Results are cached on the device. MyTok does not request your current device location for this feature. These coordinates are not sent to RevenueCat.

Purchases and membership

Apple handles in-app payment. RevenueCat processes the transaction and membership information necessary to verify purchases, maintain MyTok Pro access, restore purchases and provide purchase-related reporting. This includes an SDK-managed anonymous member identifier, purchased product information, store transaction information and necessary technical information such as app/device platform information. Network requests also expose connection information to the receiving service. We do not receive your payment-card details from Apple, or send your photos, videos, notes, tags or shooting locations to RevenueCat as member attributes or media uploads.

You may provide your support ID when asking us to investigate a membership issue. It identifies a RevenueCat customer record; it is not a password. Restoration follows the Apple purchase account and the configured RevenueCat restoration policy. An anonymous ID alone does not authenticate ownership.

RevenueCat processes membership data on our behalf and may process it in the United States and other locations used by its service providers. Apple processes its services under its own terms and privacy information.

RevenueCat privacy information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy
Apple privacy information: Apple Privacy Policy

Diagnostics and notifications

Optional MyTok usage diagnostics remain on the device. They contain basic feature counts and a bounded recent-event journal, without media, notes, tags, locations or filenames. Turning this option off clears the local diagnostic journal. This version does not upload general playback or browsing analytics. RevenueCat transaction processing is separate from this setting; its optional SDK diagnostics and marketing integrations are not enabled by the current implementation.

Memory reminders are optional and scheduled locally through iOS notifications. They can be disabled in the app or iPhone settings. Reminders do not upload your photo library to a MyTok server.

Support correspondence and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message and any information you choose to include, such as a Support ID or app version. Correspondence is handled through our email provider and used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Please do not attach private media, passwords or payment-card details for ordinary support.

Local data remains under the app's local storage rules until you remove it or it is removed with the app. Purchase and support records are kept as necessary for membership restoration, support, security and applicable legal obligations; uninstalling MyTok does not automatically erase records held by Apple or RevenueCat. We do not sell your personal information or use your photo library for advertising.

Policy and support webpages

For policy and support pages hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The pages do not include MyTok analytics scripts, advertising trackers or contact forms. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Your choices and contact

You can change photo access and notification access in iPhone Settings, change app preferences, edit or remove notes, and turn off local diagnostics. Uninstalling the app does not itself cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions through Apple.

For membership or privacy questions, contact allenfangailun@outlook.com. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights concerning information we hold about you. We may ask for proportionate information to locate the relevant record and verify the request, such as a Support ID; never send a password. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and records we must retain. We cannot directly access or delete media and notes stored only on your device.

Changes

Updates will be posted on the published policy page with an updated effective date. Material changes to data handling will be communicated as required by applicable law.
